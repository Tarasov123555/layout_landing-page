Questions

Good to know before booking.

Do I need to provide anything?

For mobile appointments, access to the vehicle and a safe working area is usually enough. If power or water is needed, we will confirm that before your appointment.

How long does detailing take?

Timing depends on vehicle size, condition, and selected services. A maintenance detail can be quicker, while paint correction or coating requires more time.

Can scratches be removed?

Many swirl marks, haze, and light scratches can be improved with paint correction. Deep scratches may remain visible if they have gone through the clear coat.

How often should I detail my vehicle?

Many drivers schedule maintenance every few months, with deeper correction or coating services when the finish needs protection or restoration.
